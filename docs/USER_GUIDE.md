# VoiceRag — User Guide

## 1. Getting Started
VoiceRag provides a conversational workspace that allows you to interact via text and natural voice in both English and Arabic while retrieving verifiable facts from your private knowledge documents.

## 2. Navigating the Workspace
- **Sidebar (Left)**:
  - **New Chat**: Starts a fresh conversational session.
  - **Recent Chats**: Switch between your active and past conversations.
  - **Knowledge Bases**: View indexed document collections and select which collection grounds your current conversation.
  - **Analytics**: View token usage and cost metrics.
  - **Settings**: Access full configuration.
- **Main Chat & Voice**:
  - Type in the message composer or click the **Microphone** icon to begin speaking.
  - While speaking, the animated waveform reflects your voice in real time.
  - When the assistant speaks, you can click **Stop Speaking** or simply talk over it (Barge-In) to interrupt.
- **Contextual Panel (Right)**:
  - Displays the active knowledge base, source documents, and detailed passage previews.
  - Click any **[Citation]** badge in an assistant answer to jump directly to the supporting page and highlight.

## 3. Language Switching
- Click the language toggle (`EN` / `AR`) in the top navigation or sidebar.
- Switching to Arabic immediately mirrors the UI to Right-to-Left (RTL), sets Arabic typography, and configures the speech synthesis for natural Arabic dialogue.
